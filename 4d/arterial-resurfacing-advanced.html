    Getting around

    Use the Navigator (top left) to jump to any location, Location 3 work zone or segment; its dots show what is happening on the current date (amber working, green done) and the figures show top course placed. Type in Go to to find a cross street or an activity. Click or drag on the minimap to move the view.
